description=Searchable, folder-based map theme browser on the Layers panel eye button — fully compatible with native map themes.
about=Replaces the flat "Manage Map Themes" menu with a popup that has instant search, nested folders, drag and drop and one-click "Update theme". Folders are just part of the theme name ("Area/Topic/Theme"), so projects stay 100% compatible with plain QGIS: the themes remain normal map themes, and the plugin's extras (custom order, open folders, highlight colors, layer visibility history) are separate project properties that QGIS ignores. English and Hungarian. Includes undo, a dockable panel, and keeps all native layer-visibility actions plus the classic menu (right-click the eye button).
