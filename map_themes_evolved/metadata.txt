about=Replaces the flat "Manage Map Themes" menu with a popup that has instant search, nested folders, drag and drop and one-click "Update theme". Folders are just part of the theme name ("Area/Topic/Theme"), so projects stay 100% compatible with plain QGIS: nothing is stored besides the normal map themes. Includes undo, a dockable panel, and keeps all native layer-visibility actions plus the classic menu (right-click the eye button).
